Advisory Board

Wisdom That Guides Us

Our mentors bring decades of expertise in wellness, healing, and mindful living. Their guidance shapes every programme and experience we create.

01
— Aromatherapy Pioneer
Dr. Blossom Kochhar
Chairperson, Blossom Kochhar Group · President, OMC Paris · Intercoiffure Mondial India

Dr. Blossom Kochhar is a pioneering figure in holistic beauty and wellness with over 35 years of experience. She is widely credited as the pioneer of Aromatherapy in India and a visionary advocate of organic living. As Chairperson of the Blossom Kochhar Group of Companies, she has built an empire around natural beauty through Blossom Kochhar Aroma Magic. She is also the founder of the Blossom Kochhar College of Creative Arts & Design (BKCCAD), skilling thousands in the beauty industry. Her leadership roles include President of Organization Mondial Coiffure (OMC), Paris; President of Intercoiffure Mondial India (ICD); and President of the National Hairdressers and Beauticians Association. She is also the Chairperson of the Beauty and Wellness Sector Skills Council of India, driving standards and empowerment across the industry.

"Healthy lifestyle and wellbeing can bring out the best beauty in you. Women must identify their strength and make conscious efforts toward growth."
02
— Padma Shri · Arjuna Awardee
Dr. Deepa Malik
Padma Shri · Arjuna Awardee · India's First Female Paralympic Archer

Dr. Deepa Malik is an acclaimed Indian Paralympic athlete and a Padma Shri awardee who has redefined the boundaries of human resilience. As India's first female Paralympic archer and a national-level Wheelchair Basketball player, she has won numerous accolades including the Arjuna Award (2012) and holds three Limca Records for her sporting achievements. Her extraordinary journey from spinal cord injury to becoming a celebrated athlete and motivational figure has inspired millions across India and beyond. She is a passionate advocate for disability rights, women's empowerment, and adaptive sports, demonstrating that true wellness transcends physical limitations.

"Every challenge is an invitation to grow stronger. Disability is not the end —  it is the beginning of discovering an even fiercer you."
03
— Holistic Wellness Visionary
Dr. Mickey Mehta
Holistic Health Scientist · Serious Ailment Management Expert · 45+ Years

Dr. Mickey Mehta is a renowned holistic healing practitioner who has devoted over four and a half decades to transforming lives across India and beyond. His integrative Serious Ailment Management programme combines curative nutrition, physiotherapy, mental-emotional wellness, Ayurveda, naturopathy, and ancient therapies to manage, stabilise, and potentially reverse chronic health conditions. Over his career, he has inspired and touched the lives of over 100 million people, guiding them from illness to wellness. Known for his philosophy that "medicine manages the condition but true healing is still missing," he bridges modern medical science with evidence-based natural therapies for comprehensive healing.

"Death has no right over life. Your breathing can regulate your hormonology. Your meditation can reshape your emotions."
04
— Padma Shri · Homeopathy Pioneer
Dr. Mukesh Batra
Padma Shri · Founder & Chairman, Dr Batra's® Positive Health Clinic

Dr. Mukesh Batra is a Padma Shri awardee and the Founder & Chairman of Dr Batra's® Positive Health Clinic, India's leading homeopathy healthcare chain with over 40 years of expertise. With 200+ clinics across India, 350+ specialist doctors, and a 91% success rate, Dr Batra's® has treated over 15 lakh patients for conditions ranging from hair and skin disorders to chronic ailments including respiratory, women's health, child health, and mental wellness. He combines classical homeopathy with modern diagnostic technology to deliver personalised, safe, and effective treatment. Trusted by Bollywood icons including Shah Rukh Khan, Hrithik Roshan, and Sonakshi Sinha, he continues to pioneer evidence-based homeopathic care across India.

"Homeopathy treats the root cause, not just the symptoms —  delivering lasting relief with no side effects."
05
— Real Estate Icon
Dr. Niranjan Hiranandani
Founder & Chairman, Hiranandani Group · Chairman, NAREDCO · Past President, ASSOCHAM

Dr. Niranjan Hiranandani is widely recognised as one of India's pioneering business titans and a thought leader who reshaped the nation's urban development landscape. As the Founder & Chairman of the Hiranandani Group, he is credited with redefining integrated township living through landmark developments such as Hiranandani Gardens, Powai. A first-generation entrepreneur and Fellow Chartered Accountant, he has over five decades of experience driving sustainable urban planning, infrastructure development, and economic growth across India.

"Cities must be built around people, communities, and long-term vision —  not just addresses, but economies."
06
— Tarot Queen & Numerologist
Dr. Seema Midha
Internationally Renowned Tarot Expert · Ace Numerologist · Feng Shui Master

Based in New Delhi, Dr. Seema Midha is an internationally renowned Tarot card expert, teacher, and ace numerologist with a mission to transform lives through ancient wisdom and psychic knowledge. A Post Graduate in Child Psychology, she is also specialised in Feng Shui, Vaastu, Colour therapy, Crystal ball gazing, Reiki, Karuna Reiki, Pranic Healing, Acupressure, Psychotherapy, Hypnotism, Aura Reading, and Angel Healing. She has been featured extensively across electronic media including TV shows and radio, and print publications. She empowers individuals with self-confidence and guides them toward happiness and success through precise numerological calculations and tarot readings.

"There is no better boat than a horoscope to help a man cross over the sea of life."
07
— Green Building Authority
Gurmit Singh Arora
Co-Chair, IGBC Mumbai · Managing Director, Rajco Group · National Executive Council Member, Indian Plumbing Association

Gurmit Singh Arora is a highly respected name in India's sustainable building and plumbing industry. As Managing Director of Rajco Group —  a leading manufacturing company near Mumbai —  he has achieved milestones that have made him an authority on copper tubes, with Rajco holding an 85% market share. He is a Founding Trustee of the Indian Institute of Plumbing, Founding & Executive Council Member and Co-Chairman of the Indian Green Building Council (IGBC) Mumbai Chapter, and Chairman of the IGBC Existing Buildings, O&M Steering Committee. A National Executive Council Member of the Indian Plumbing Association, he has been honoured by the Builders Association of India, Union Bank of India, Hitachi, and numerous other organisations for excellence and innovation. He actively promotes green building practices and sustainable infrastructure across India.

"Sustainability is not a choice anymore —  it is an imperative. Every building we create today must serve generations tomorrow."

Our Why

A Vision Born From Personal Transformation

We believe that every individual carries the seed of Sattva within them —  purity, balance, and inner harmony. Our mission is to create spaces where that seed can grow.

Sattva Nation was born from a simple yet profound realization: in the rush of modern life, people have forgotten how to pause. Our founders and mentors came together with a shared vision —  to bring holistic wellness out of exclusive retreats and into everyday life, making mindful living accessible to all.
