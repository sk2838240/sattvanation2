Our Approach

Practical, not classroom.

A first of its kind wellness festival in India, Sattva Nation focuses on both external as well as internal development and wellness. The knowledge to attain the same is imparted in an actual open environment with focus on practical implementation rather than the boring classroom format.

We take on the challenges of modern life such as stress, anxiety, and depression —  and help you overcome these by living your life to the best of your potential.

Who We Are

A holistic wellness movement

Sattva Nation is a holistic wellness movement dedicated to nurturing the complete well-being of individuals. Rooted in balance, mindfulness and purposeful living, Sattva brings together experts, organisations and communities to inspire healthier, happier and more meaningful lives.

Through events, wellness experiences, educational initiatives, corporate programs and community engagements, Sattva promotes growth across every dimension of human wellness.
